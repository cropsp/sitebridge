## What Makes a Gag Gift Actually Good

After testing (and receiving) dozens of gag gifts over the years, I've developed a simple test:

1. **Is it funny?** Obvious, but skip the mean-spirited stuff. The best gag gifts laugh *with* the recipient, not *at* them.

2. **Is it useful?** The money clip wins here. The grill apron wins here. The joke button... well, it's useful for annoying people. That counts.

3. **Would they actually keep it?** If the answer is no, you're buying future landfill. Spend a few extra dollars on something that sticks around.

The Cheeky Money Clip passes all three. It's genuinely well-made leather goods with a sense of humor — which, honestly, is rarer than it should be. Most leather brands take themselves deadly seriously. Lamamarka doesn't, and the product is better for it.
